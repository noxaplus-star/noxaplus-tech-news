# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

NoxaPlus Tech News is an open-source project. We want participation to be useful and pleasant for everyone, regardless of experience, background, identity, or language. Contributors are expected to treat each other with respect in issues, pull requests, discussions, and any project space.

## Expected behaviour

- Discuss ideas and code, not people.
- Give specific, actionable feedback and accept it gracefully.
- Assume good faith; ask before escalating.
- Respect bilingual work: do not dismiss Persian copy, RTL layout, or accessibility concerns as optional.
- Credit sources, collaborators, and prior work.
- Keep issues and pull requests on topic.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination of any kind.
- Personal attacks, insults, or sustained disruption.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Plagiarism, fabricated reporting presented as fact, or removing the project's demo-content disclosure to mislead readers.
- Bad-faith security disclosures or attempts to compromise the hosted demo or its visitors.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces — the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, and any communication made in the project's name — and when an individual represents the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close threads, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently ban participants whose behaviour violates this code. Decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, and focused on repairing harm.

To report a concern, email **NoxaPlus2025@gmail.com** with a description and any supporting context. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers with a conflict of interest will step aside from the decision.
